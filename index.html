About Me

Passion Meets Precision

I'm Palak Tailor, a dedicated Mehndi artist based in Surat with over 7 years of experience crafting intricate henna designs for brides, festivals, and every occasion in between.

From delicate bridal designs to bold Arabic patterns and playful party mehndi, I bring artistry and care to every stroke. My henna is always 100% natural — safe, fragrant, and rich in colour.

My goal is simple: to make every client feel like the most beautiful version of themselves on their special day.
